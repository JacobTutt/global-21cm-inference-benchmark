[`LICENSES/BSD-3-Clause.txt`](LICENSES/BSD-3-Clause.txt).

The pretrained radio-galaxy emulator parameters in
`src/global21cm_benchmark/emulator/weights.npz` are excluded from that grant.
They are distributed under the custom 21cmSPACE Benchmark Model Use Licence
(`LicenseRef-21cmSPACE-Benchmark-Use-1.0`) provided in
[`LICENSES/LicenseRef-21cmSPACE-Benchmark-Use-1.0.txt`](LICENSES/LicenseRef-21cmSPACE-Benchmark-Use-1.0.txt).
The model-use licence permits reproduction and methodological benchmarking but
requires prior written permission from the 21cmSPACE collaboration before the
emulator is used to derive independent astrophysical or cosmological results.

The custom model-use terms mean that the pretrained emulator parameters are
source-available but are not open source under the Open Source Definition.
Questions and permission requests should be directed to Professor Anastasia
Fialkov at <afialkov@ast.cam.ac.uk>.

Copyright and licensing metadata for the binary weights are also recorded in
the adjacent `weights.npz.license` sidecar following the REUSE specification.
